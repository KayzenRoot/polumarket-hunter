# v0.1 evidence and strategy decision

Status: **owner approval pending**.
Research date: 2026-10-26.
Repository commit for the research tooling: `4d91f07` plus the validator/recorder fixes in this
memo's PR.
Strategy under test: `micro-maker` with the default strategy and risk configuration.

This memo is evidence for the default paper strategy. It is not financial advice and it does not
claim that prediction-market prices are objective probabilities.

## Method

1. Recorded three independent public-book sessions with `npm run record`.
2. Validated every recording with `npm run data:validate` before any backtest.
3. Ran `npm run research` with a 70/30 time split and an explicit fee assumption:
   `rate 0.05 × (p × (1-p))^1`, taker-only.
4. Compared the strategy with the always-reported no-trade and passive-hold baselines.
5. Did not tune on the validation partition.

The fee assumption is deliberately conservative for the mixed market set. Some recorded markets may
be fee-free or in lower-fee categories; a per-market schedule was not available in the JSONL format,
so the session-level curve is the documented assumption for all three runs. The same config hash
(`eacae277...`) covers all three research reports.

Aggregate strategy PnL across the three train partitions: **−$2.42**. Aggregate validation PnL:

−$0.03.

Passive-hold lost 11–32% in every partition, so this was not a “do nothing and win” regime. It also
means the strategy did not add value over directional exposure; it merely avoided most of the
passive losses by trading rarely.

## What the evidence supports

- The paper engine, risk gates, recorder, validator and research harness work end to end on real
  public data. This is production evidence for the **tooling**.
- The default `micro-maker` configuration does **not** show a verified edge. It is not profitable
  after the stated fee assumption, it trades rarely in validation, and its win rate is poor.
- No-trade beat the strategy in every session. The strategy did not beat no-trade after fees.
- The sample is small and same-day. It is enough to reject a claim of edge; it is not enough to
  establish a durable negative edge.

## Decision

**Recommendation: NO-GO for promoting the default strategy as having an edge or enabling live
trading. GO for v0.1.0 as a local, single-user, paper-first research terminal.**

Concretely:

- Keep live execution blocked; the geoblock decision and v0.2.0 boundary remain unchanged.
- Label the default strategy as unvalidated in the UI/docs; do not advertise returns.
- Keep the research harness and fee-aware reporting as the production evidence path.
- The next hypothesis should be tested on **train only** with a new config hash, then confirmed on a
  fresh out-of-sample session. Do not tune against these validation numbers.
- A sensible next hypothesis is maker-only entry/exit with fee-aware edge thresholds; the current
  taker exits pay the curve and the 5% assumption is material for a scalper. This is a hypothesis,
  not a result.

## Limitations

- Three sessions on one day, mostly the same liquid market set; session 3 used five higher-liquidity
  markets.
- Session-level 5% fee approximation; per-market schedules and maker rebates are not in the
  recording format.
- No queue position, latency, partial-fill realism beyond the simulator, or market impact.
- The validator warnings (crossed snapshots, small clock skew, one 49s gap) are documented but not
  eliminated.
- The sample is not statistically powered. Do not present these numbers as expected future returns.

## Reproduction

```bash
npm run data:validate -- --file data/recordings/<session>.jsonl
npm run research -- --file data/recordings/<session>.jsonl --fee-rate 0.05 --out data/reports
```

The reports include the config hash, fee assumption, split windows, baseline metrics and equity
curves. The full JSON is deterministic for the same input/config.
