# Maker-exit hypothesis — train evidence

Status: **train gate passed; out-of-sample blocked by local DNS. No promotion.**
Date: 2026-10-28.
Strategy: `maker-scalper` (maker bid entry, maker ask take-profit, taker only for stop/edge decay).
Frozen config hash: `65b2f1adfb7c11a53a0bfdac0fb09f177237d87cc9e34030583fe0d4fc44e66e`.

This memo is research evidence, not investment advice. It does not claim that prediction-market
prices are objective probabilities.

## Hypothesis

The default micro-maker loses mainly because taker exits pay the fee curve. A maker-entry/maker-exit
variant with a fee-aware profit target should reduce fees and improve net PnL without increasing risk
beyond the existing limits. Taker exits remain only as a stop-loss or edge-decay safety valve.

`stopLossEdge=0.015` point. No old validation partition was used for selection.

## Limitations

- The train sample is the same three 2026-10-26 sessions; the grid is small and the selected point
  may be overfit.
- Maker fills assume the simulated queue and do not model queue position or adverse selection.
- The 5% session-level fee is an assumption; per-market schedules are not in the recording format.
- No fresh data was available, so the out-of-sample claim is absent.
- No live order path, wallet or geoblock bypass was exercised.
